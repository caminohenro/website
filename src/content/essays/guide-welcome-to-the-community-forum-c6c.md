I wanted to create a community forum where people could start discussions and ask questions related to the pilgrimage. However, Substack doesn’t natively support a full-fledged community forum—the closest thing is the Chat function which is not exactly what I’m looking for,—so we have to do some workarounds.

## The System

To post on each other’s publications, you have to be added as a **collaborator**. Trouble is, collaborators have a lot of control over the publication, so I can’t just gave everyone who subscribes collaborator perks. The next best thing is **bylines** / guest authors, which can be added as authors but can’t actually post things directly. What we’re going to do is a mix between collaborators and bylines to get people’s posts on the message board.

## For new members

When you first enter the forum, you can reply to other people’s posts, but you can’t post directly by yourself yet.

To submit a post request, you **fill in the following Google Forms** with the post details.

This form asks for your name or substack @handle. This lets admins add your name as **guest author** to the post, provided you accept an invitation that will be sent to you by email.

## For trusted members

If you’re getting serious about participating in this Forum, the first thing you might want to do is subscribe.

Once you’ve been part of the community for a while, and show your trustworthiness, I may add you as a collaborator. That will allow you to post your own threads as well as other people’s requests.

#### How to post:

1.  Go to the **Dashboard** on the top right corner of this page right here ↗ (only available after I added you);
    
2.  Press **Create** and then select **Article**.
    
3.  _(If you’re posting for other people):_ Just below the title and subtitle you will see your name and a (+) button. **Click the (+) button** and write the name of the person who you’re posting for.
    
4.  Now just write the title, subtitle and actual post. Simple, right!
    
5.  Once you’re done writing, you can hit **Continue (**again, to the top right ↗) and select any **tags** that fit your post. You can also select whether or not to deliver an email to subscribers’ emails.
    

Being a collaborator allows you to post under other people’s name, so it’s very important that only trusted people have that power. I will add you as collaborator if you submit posts often enough, or if you request to be added.
