## Kon’nichiwa!

##### （こんにちは）

Welcome to my blog and website. I am **Henro, a pilgrim** on a quest to cross the entire landmass of Eurasia on foot while sustaining myself entirely on the providence of the _camino_. I started the journey in Portugal, my home country at the western tip of Europe, following the Portuguese route of the Camino de Santiago up to Finisterra as an initial ‘trial’. There I met **Kasa, the hat**. From there, I have continued my journey on foot across the two continents, heading towards Japan, where I intend to complete the **Shikoku pilgrimage** and return Kasa to its original home.

## Ok, but what is this blog about?

This publication will have **three sections:**

#### Blog

The main section is called the _Blog_. Here, I will be writing **philosophical essays** related to the topic of **security.** My mission is to inspire people all over the world to break free from the fear of unemployment and open their eyes to different possibilities for finding security in your life.

If you find yourself struggling to keep up with life, working hard to pay the bills, stress piling up and your dreams slowly going down the drain—or getting pushed back into the promised land of retirement—hopefully this will help you put on a different pair of goggles with which to see life, discover new possibilities and summon the necessary creativity to change things in your favour.

The essays in this substack will also be available on the main website.

<div class="substack-imported-btn-wrap" style="text-align: center; margin: 24px 0;"><a href="https://caminohenro.substack.com/s/blog" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="ink-action-btn" style="background: var(--accent-crimson); color: #F4F1EA; border-color: var(--accent-crimson);">Explore the Blog ↗</a></div>

#### Newsletter

This is a **monthly** newsletter where I’ll be sharing **updates and highlights** from the journey itself. You will learn about where I am, what I’m doing, and what’s happened since last month. These newsletters are the only thing I will be archiving under a paywall, as they are meant to be live updates on the current state of the journey, not permanent posts.

Every subscriber to this section will receive **new issues for free** on their email, but the archive itself will be available to paid subscribers as a token of gratitude.

#### Forum

The final section is the **Forum**, a place for the community to share questions, insights and provocative thoughts. There, you’ll be able to **submit your own posts** for starting discussions. This requires some working around Substacks limitations, so be sure to check the [Guide post](https://caminohenro.substack.com/p/guide-welcome-to-the-community-forum-c6c?r=9g30s7) to learn more about how this works.

It is my hope that my quest will inspire a lot of questions, judgements and skepticism on your part, so the invitation is that you bring that as a discussion and **build a community** together for exploring these deep topiccs that touch everyone’s lives.

## Ready to embark?

All of my essays will be **public and freely available.** Paid subscriptions will be a gift that you may choose to give — not to access exclusive content, but out of a genuine wish to support my writing and journey. If I do write any content for paid subscribers, it will be things like gratitude notes or special stories about funny happenings from the road. Everything related to the main mission of this blog will always be freely available, so you will never miss out on any “important” essays.
