## Kon’nichiwa!

##### （こんにちは）

Welcome to my blog and website. I am **Henro, a pilgrim** on a quest to cross the entire landmass of Eurasia on foot while sustaining myself entirely on the providence of the _camino_. I started the journey in Portugal, my home country at the western tip of Europe, following the Portuguese route of the Camino de Santiago up to Finisterra as an initial ‘trial’. There I met **Kasa, the hat**. From there, I have continued my journey on foot across the two continents, heading towards Japan, where I intend to complete the **Shikoku pilgrimage** and return Kasa to its original home.

## Ok, but what is this blog about?

This blog is not the place where I’ll be describing the highlights of what happened on the journey. There will be a separate monthly newsletter for that, which you can subscribe to on my **main website**.

In this blog, I will be writing **philosophical essays** related to the notion of **security**: What it means to me during this journey, my sources of sustenance, my strategies to keep going, etc.. My mission is to provide inspiration for people from all over the world to break free from the fear of unemployment and seeing different possibilities for where you can find security in your life.

If you find yourself struggling to keep up with life, working hard to pay the bills, stress piling up and your dreams slowly going down the drain — or getting pushed back into the promised land of retirement… — hopefully this will help you put on a different pair of goggles with which to see life in a different way, discover new possibilities and summon the necessary creativity to change things in your favour.

The essays in this substack will also be available on the website. The main purpose of this Substack is to allow for interested readers to receive email notifications whenever a new essay is published, as well as helping new readers find their way to Camino Henro with Substack’s '“discovery” feature.

---

## Ready to embark?

In principle, all of my essays will be public and freely available. Paid subscriptions will be a gift that you may choose to give — not to access exclusive content, but out of a genuine wish to support my writing and journey. If I do any content for paid subscribers, it will be things like gratitude notes or special stories about funny happenings from the road. Everything related to the main mission of this blog will always be freely available, so you will never miss out on any “important” essays.
