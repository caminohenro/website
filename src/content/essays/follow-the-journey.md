# Newsletter

I consciously decided not to use Instagram—not in my life, and not for this journey. But I still want to give people these updates, so I decided to start a **Newsletter**.

To receive these monthly updates, you should [subscribe](https://caminohenro.substack.com/subscribe). Subscribing puts you on both the Blog and the Newsletter mailing lists, but you can opt out of either in under your [account settings](https://caminohenro.substack.com/account).
